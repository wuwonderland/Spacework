## 🚫 DISPUTED CLAIMS (3)

### DISPUTE 1: Foreign Buyer Share — Tokyo 23 Wards
- **Source A:** Ministry of Justice survey (July 2025) — 3.0% of new condo buyers have overseas addresses
- **Source B:** Mitsubishi UFJ Trust developer survey (2025) — 18.5% of buyers in central wards are foreign nationals
- **Explanation:** SOURCES MEASURE DIFFERENT THINGS. MOJ tracks registered overseas address; MUFG survey likely tracks nationality or investment vehicle type. These are fundamentally different definitions. Both figures are correct within their respective methodologies.
- **Status:** DISPUTED — methodological difference acknowledged, no resolution attempted.

### DISPUTE 2: Tokyo Office Vacancy Rate
- **Source A:** CBRE Q2 2026 — All-Grade vacancy: 1.4%
- **Source B:** Real Estate Tokyo Summary (2025) — All-Grade vacancy: 1.6%
- **Explanation:** DIFFERENT OBSERVATION PERIODS. CBRE covers Q2 2026; the other covers Q4 2025.
- **Status:** DISPUTED — temporal difference acknowledged. Most recent (CBRE) preferred for current analysis.

### DISPUTE 3: Osaka Office Vacancy Rate

- **Explanation:** DIFFERENT OBSERVATION PERIODS. CBRE more recent (Q2 2026).
- **Status:** DISPUTED — temporal difference acknowledged. Most recent (CBRE) preferred.
